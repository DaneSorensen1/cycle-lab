Reference: the two rules and the three questions (tap to open)
Rule 1. Cash is never used in an adjusting entry.
Rule 2. Every adjusting entry has exactly one income statement account (a revenue or an expense) and one balance sheet account (an asset or a liability).
1. What can we recognize? Name the revenue or expense account, and the amount.
2. Has the cash already moved? Yes: the other account is already on the books — find it, and it comes down (Prepaid ___, Supplies, Deferred Revenue, or the book value of equipment through Accumulated Depreciation). No: add a ___ Payable or Accounts Receivable.
3. Which side? An expense is a debit. A revenue is a credit. The balance sheet account takes the other side.

Net income counts revenue when it is earned and expenses when they are incurred. Cash counts money when it moves. Assets and expenses go up with a debit; liabilities, equity and revenue go up with a credit.
